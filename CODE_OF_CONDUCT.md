# Code of conduct

This project wants to be a place where anyone can contribute without being
harassed or put down, whatever their background, experience or identity.

**Expected:** be kind and direct, criticise code and ideas rather than people,
accept that others may decide differently, and help newcomers.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, sexual attention or imagery, threats,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break
these rules, and may ban a person from the project for it, temporarily or for
good. This applies in all project spaces, and wherever someone represents the
project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/marselnenaj). Reports are treated
confidentially.
